FAQ

Questions, answered.

Can't find what you need? Our team replies within one business day.

Who needs a POSH Internal Committee?

Any organization with 10 or more employees must constitute an Internal Committee under the POSH Act. We set it up, train members and handle annual filings.

Can you manage payroll end-to-end?

Yes — salary processing, statutory deductions, PF/ESI/TDS returns, payslips and employee queries, with full audit trails.

Do you work with startups as well as large enterprises?

Absolutely. We scale from a first HR policy for a 15-person team to multi-location compliance programmes for large organizations.

How quickly can an engagement start?

After a discovery call we typically share a scoped proposal within 3 business days and begin onboarding the following week.
